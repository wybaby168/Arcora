Arcora RAR local evaluation build / RAR 本机验证构建

This locally assembled app is for private local use and integration testing,
not customer distribution. It contains official RAR 7.23 engines, not a license.
The RARLAB maximum 40-day evaluation period and usage terms apply:
https://www.rarlab.com/license.htm
Continued use requires your own valid license. Import your purchased rarreg.key
in Settings > Engines. Rebuilding does not restart or extend evaluation.
No redistribution permission or customer usage rights are granted by Arcora.
The app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or Apple-notarized.
Do not publish, sell, or forward this app as a customer-ready installation package.
